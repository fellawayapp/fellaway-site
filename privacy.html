Fellaway Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Fellaway helps you turn a trip into a story and share it with friends you choose. This policy explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it. We've tried to keep it short and plain.

Who we are: Fellaway is an independent app ("we", "us"). Questions or requests, including asking what we hold about you: hello@fellaway.app.

The short version

  • Your trips are private by default. A trip stays on your phone unless you share it.
  • When you share a trip, only the friends you pick can see it, and you can stop at any time.
  • Reviews are the exception: they're public. If you post a review of a place, other Fellaway users can see it. Your trips stay private.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.
  • You can delete your account inside the app. That deletes your data from our servers.

What we collect

Account details. Your email address and a password (we never see your password; it's stored in a scrambled form by our sign-in provider), the name and handle you choose, and an invite code we generate for you. If we add Sign in with Apple, we receive the identifier Apple provides, and your name and email if you choose to share them.

Your trips. Trip names and dates, moments (titles, descriptions, places, side quests), flights you add (airline and flight number, and airports if you enter them), places you stayed (a name, and its map position if you pick it from a search), the background you choose for a trip's path, your rating of a finished trip (and an optional one-line tip), photos and captions. Until you share a trip, all of this stays on your phone. When you share a trip, a copy is uploaded to our servers so your friends can view it.

Photos and their location. Photos you add may carry the place and time they were taken. If a trip is shared, that location and time information is stored with the photo and can be seen by the friends you shared it with. Photos are resized before they're uploaded.

Your photo library. When you add photos to a moment, Fellaway can look through your phone's photo library to show you the photos taken on that moment's day first. This happens only on your phone: the library is never uploaded, and we never see which photos you looked at. Only photos you choose to add to a moment are copied into the app. If you'd rather not give access, you can choose "Don't Allow" or "Limit Access" and pick photos with Apple's own photo picker instead.

Places and the shared catalog. Every moment is tagged with a place (and, if you like, what you did there, such as a concert or a round of golf). When you tag a place, we store its name, type and map coordinates with the moment. Places and experiences are also added to a shared catalog that all Fellaway users can search, so that everyone's "Holywood Golf Club" is the same one. The catalog holds only the place (name, type, map position), not who tagged it, when, or which trip it was part of. You can mark a place as private (for example your home) so it stays in your trip and never enters the catalog. We keep a private record of who first added each catalog entry, only to deal with abuse. If you see a wrong or inappropriate catalog entry, you can report it, or email hello@fellaway.app. If you tap "suggest nearby places", the app uses your phone's location once to search Apple Maps for nearby places. We don't keep a record of where you are.

Reviews. After you save a moment, the app offers to review the place (and what you did there). A review is optional and has a rating, a few quick answers (such as what it's good for or what to order), one short note, and, if you choose, one of your photos from that moment. Unlike your trips, reviews are public: every signed-in Fellaway user can see them, along with the date of your visit and a link to your account, so people can tell reviews apart and, in future, find reviews from friends. We don't publish the rest of your trip. A review is published once its place is in the shared catalog. You can edit or delete a review at any time (open the moment and tap the review), and deleting the moment or the trip takes down its reviews and their photos. Deleting your account deletes all your reviews and review photos.

Your points and Traveler number. Your points total (a number your phone works out from what's in your trips) is stored with your profile so we can show your Traveler number compared with everyone else on Fellaway. Friends you've accepted can see your points total and number. What earned the points isn't uploaded, and private trips stay private.

Profile picture. If you add one, we store it on our servers so your friends can see it next to your name. It's optional, and you can remove it any time.

Friends. Who you've sent or accepted friend requests with, and who you've blocked.

Reminders. The evening reminder during a trip is scheduled by your phone itself; nothing about it is sent to us. You can turn it off in your profile.

Notifications about friends. If you allow notifications, we store an address for your phone (a "device token" that Apple provides) so we can tell you when a friend adds to a trip they've shared with you, shares a trip with you, sends you a friend request or accepts yours, for example "Danny just added to Galway". The notification contains the friend's name and, where relevant, the trip name and the moment's title and place. Reminders to add moments or rate a trip are scheduled on your phone and don't involve our servers. We use Apple's push notification service to deliver it, and we keep a short record of when we last notified you about a trip so you aren't sent many in a row. You can turn these off in your profile, or in your phone's settings, and signing out removes your phone's address.

Finding people. Anyone who uses Fellaway can search for people by name or handle to send a friend request. A search shows only a person's name and handle (never their email, invite code, picture or trips), and never shows someone who has blocked you. You can switch off "Let people find me by name" in your profile; then only someone who types your exact handle, or has your invite code, can find you.

Photo suggestions. Caption ideas for photos are worked out on your phone. Your photos aren't sent anywhere for this.

Who can see what

  • Only you can see your private trips.
  • Friends you've accepted and chosen to share a trip with can view that whole trip, including its photos. They can't change it.
  • Friends you've accepted can also see your profile picture, if you've added one, and your points total and Traveler number.
  • A person who finds you by searching your name or handle, or who has your invite code, can send you a friend request and sees only your name and handle. They can't see anything else until you accept. You can switch off being found by name in your profile.
  • Everyone who uses Fellaway can find a place or experience in the shared catalog (its name, type and map position), but not who tagged it or anything about your trips.
  • Everyone who uses Fellaway can also see the reviews you post, including the photo you attach to a review, but nothing else from the trip they came from.
  • Blocked people can't find you or send you requests.
  • We (the people running Fellaway) can technically access stored data to run and secure the service, but we don't look at your trips except when needed to fix a problem or follow the law.

Who we share data with

We use service providers to run Fellaway: Supabase (database, photo storage and sign-in, hosted on Amazon Web Services servers in the United States) and Apple (the App Store, TestFlight, Sign in with Apple, and Apple Maps). They process data on our behalf and only to provide their service. We don't sell or rent your data to anyone, and we may disclose information if the law requires it.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored on servers in the United States (Northern Virginia). If you use Fellaway from another country, your data is transferred to and stored in the United States.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. If you stop sharing a trip, its shared copy is removed from our servers. If you delete a trip, it is removed from your phone and from our servers. If you delete your account, we delete your profile and picture, friendships, shared trips, reviews and uploaded photos. Our hosting provider may keep encrypted backups for a limited time after deletion.

Your choices

  • Share or stop sharing any trip at any time (tap the person icon on a trip, or press and hold it, then "Share with friends").
  • Remove or block a friend at any time. Removing them also stops them seeing any trip you shared.
  • Delete your account in the app: Trips tab, tap your picture and name at the top, then "Delete my account".
  • To ask what we hold about you or to correct it, email hello@fellaway.app.

Children

Fellaway is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has made an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, stored on managed infrastructure, and protected by rules that only let people read what's been shared with them. No service is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes

If we change how Fellaway uses your data, for example by adding features that use your content more widely, we'll update this policy and ask for your agreement before using existing content in a new way.
